For more than half a century, experiential education has shaped learners, strengthened communities, and inspired new approaches to teaching and engagement. This year’s theme celebrates and honors that legacy while looking ahead to the future — exploring how community-engaged/service learning, work-integrated learning, research, and other immersive practices continue to drive meaningful, inclusive, and innovative learning experiences.

What to Expect at the 2026 SEE Annual Conference
A milestone celebration
Reflecting on 55 years of leadership, collaboration, and transformative practice across the experiential education community.

A dynamic conference format
A schedule designed to deepen learning, spark conversation, and create space for meaningful connection.

A robust program of sessions
Featuring a wide range of topics in experiential education — including the return of Poster Sessions.
