You're invited to join the Association for Experiential Education at 'ASCEND: Growing Community, Elevating Practice', the 54th Annual International Experiential Education Conference taking place November 12-14, 2026, at The YMCA of the Rockies in Estes Park, Colorado. 

With hundreds of educators, students, practitioners, and researchers in attendance, you'll have the opportunity to learn from experts and innovators, develop lasting professional and personal connections, enrich your work or studies, and discover what's possible in the industry. The conference features over 80 workshops, research sessions, and panels, alongside keynote events and networking sessions. Nestled within 860+ acres of spectacular vistas and ponderosa pines, the YMCA of the Rockies provides an inspiring backdrop for the global experiential education community to gather, share knowledge, and explore the latest research and innovative methodologies.
