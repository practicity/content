The Practice Enterprise community will gather online from 5 to 9 October 2026 for the PEN Worldwide International Training Conference, a new event designed to bring together Central Office staff, trainers, and educational innovators from around the world. This conference creates a space dedicated to experiential learning, entrepreneurship and the continued evolution of the Practice Enterprise concept. 

Attendees will have the opportunity to engage in sessions focused on refreshing knowledge, teaching new skills, and exploring the changing landscape of the labor market through a competency and skill-based approach. The event is an inclusive opportunity for the global network to collaborate on international projects and exchange best practices.
