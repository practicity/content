The Future of Education Technology Conference (FETC) includes a specialized focus on Career and Technical Education through its dedicated CTE Pavilion and program tracks. This segment of the expo is designed for educators and administrators looking to bridge the gap between the classroom and the workforce.

The CTE Pavilion highlights the intersection of technology and vocational training, featuring hands-on demonstrations in robotics, coding, 3D printing, and immersive VR/AR simulations. Attendees can explore innovative solutions for high-demand fields and discover strategies to equip students with the industry-recognized certifications and technical skills necessary for the future of work. With dedicated sessions on workforce development and experiential learning, the CTE focus at FETC provides a comprehensive look at how technology is transforming career-based education.
